Reading the numbers: Krippendorff's α and Cohen's/Fleiss' κ measure inter-annotator agreement beyond chance (1 = perfect, 0 ≈ chance, <0 = systematic disagreement; ≥0.667 is a common acceptability threshold). Pearson/Spearman measure linear/rank correlation between annotators. Span F1 is the overlap agreement between annotators (1 = identical): the character variant counts overlapping characters, while the token variant counts overlapping whole words (more forgiving of off-by-a-character boundary differences). Both treat "neither marked anything here" as agreement. Post-editing edit distance is the share of characters changed when correcting an output (0 = left untouched, 1 = fully rewritten); lower means the system needed less fixing. Treat all agreement figures cautiously when the overlapping-sample count is small.
