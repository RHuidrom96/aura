A.U.R.A

Annotation Utility for Reliable Assessment

A.U.R.A. is created primarily for the languages, overlooked by the world, time and again. The Indigenous and Low-resource languages carry entire worlds of sounds, letters, words, meanings, structures and systems. Yet, these languages rarely encounter the computational tools that could support their development. A.U.R.A. helps curate reliable human judgments from native speakers, extract correlation stats, compare agreements and disagreements, generate reliable evaluation data real-time to build more accurate and equitable technology for underrepresented languages.
